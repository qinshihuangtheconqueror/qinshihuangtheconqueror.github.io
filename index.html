About

I am an AI Full-stack Engineer based in Hanoi, Vietnam, focused on building production LLM systems end to end—spanning agentic backends, hybrid RAG pipelines, and the guardrails that keep them grounded.

At Infratech JSC, I engineer event-driven withdrawal and payout processing for the Chocolate Finance serverless banking platform on AWS with TypeScript and fp-ts, building on earlier software and AI backend internships at Viettel High Tech and FPT Software.

For my Computer Science graduation thesis at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST, Class of 2026), I architected HUSTVA—an agentic academic chatbot evaluated over a 4-week production pilot across ~3,900 students and 54k+ conversations with 88.9% user satisfaction and 90.3% routing accuracy—alongside KMS, a multi-tenant knowledge management and hybrid retrieval platform.
